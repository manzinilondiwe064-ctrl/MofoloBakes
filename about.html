About Bella

Bella started baking in Soweto in 2020 during lockdown. What started as family birthday cakes became Bella's Bakes - a luxury custom cake studio. Every cake is made with love, fresh ingredients, and 72 hours notice. From Midnight Gold Drip to Red Velvet Royal, Bella believes every celebration deserves a dream cake.

Inside Our Kitchen

Meet Bella Khumalo - Founder
Bella is a self-taught baker from Mofolo, Soweto. She uses free-range eggs, farm milk, and Belgian chocolate. Her kitchen is where every slice tells a golden story. All cakes are handcrafted, made-to-order, and baked fresh in small batches - Quality Over Quantity.

🎯 Our Mission

To create pure, unforgettable moments through handcrafted luxury bakes that celebrate love, culture, and Soweto pride. Every cake is made with faith and gold standard service.

👁️ Our Vision

To become a global sustainable luxury cake brand by 2030, putting Soweto on the world map for luxury patisserie while empowering local women bakers.
